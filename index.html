    TIVA & target-controlled infusion

    The science behind intravenous anaesthesia.

    As Vice President of World SIVA, Prof. Shalabi champions total intravenous anaesthesia. With target-controlled infusion, the anaesthetist sets a target concentration and the pump calculates the infusion rate. Try it below.

    Career

    Trained in Alexandria and the UK. Practised on three continents.

    Foundation

    Alexandria University, Egypt

    UK training
